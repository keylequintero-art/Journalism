Journalism

La Local Youth Journalism

    Grade: Middle and High school
    Format: In person and hybrid
    Requirements: Vary by program or cohort.
    Summary: Students learn journalism and all the skills necessary along with digital media, while producing work about issues affecting their communities

Student Reporting Lab

    Format: In person
    Grade requirements: Middle school & High school
    Application period: Late September- October 27 (may vary)
    Duration: 5 days
    Summary: Students get to learn about journalism and media by creating their own articles which can then be posted on PBS’s platform

Jcal Student Reporter Summer Camp

    Format: In person
    Grade requirements: High school students
    Other requirements: Short answer questions in the application, reporting pitch, and letter of recommendation
    Application period: closes late February
    Duration: 5 days (includes virtual workshops)
    Summary: Allows students to immerse themselves into California’s news and civic systems by teaching them how to report and create stories of their local communities.

LA Times HS Insider 2 programs

1. Format: In person
    Grade requirements: High school students
    Other requirements: Must have a teacher or advisor who can act as a point of contact and a signed permission slip to set up a publishing account.
    Application period: Always open
    Duration: Ongoing and flexible to students' careers.
    Summary: Free, ongoing digital publishing network where high schoolers pitch and submit content to the L.A. Times website.Designed to build a writing portfolio at your own pace during the school year.
2. Format: Hybrid
    Grade requirements: High school students
    Other requirements: Must commit to the hybrid schedule (9:00 am - 4:00 pm), and be legally authorized to work in the U.S.
    Application period: Opens in January and closes around mid-April
    Duration: 6-7 weeks
    Pay: $21/hour
    Summary: Paid summer internship that allows students to intern under the direct mentorship of professional journalists to produce news coverage and daily enterprise.
